---
name: shorts-format
description: How a PRM Short must look. Use when writing Writer prompts, render presets, captions or QC rules.
---
- Sources: streamers' own footage (Twitch clips, Twitch/YouTube live VODs). Competitor Shorts are studied for format only (hook style, fonts, caption position, length, pacing, titles) and never downloaded or re-uploaded.
- Copy the winning *format*, not the file: our own cut, our own captions, our own context line.
- 20–45 s target, hard cap 60 s. First 1–2 s = the hook moment, not setup.
- Top: hook text (≤7 words). Under it for ~8 s: context line (who/why/what just happened, ≤12 words). Middle-lower: word-pop captions.
- Title ≤70 chars, names the streamer. No misleading claims.
- Never: speed-up, pitch shift, mirroring, heavy filters, crop tricks to dodge Content ID.
