# Format Library (updated 2026-10-20)
Use for titles and structure only. Make original edits from permitted sources. Never re-upload competitor videos.

## Hook templates (by vph, best first)
1. `{S} [absurd thing], but [twist]..`: challenge or constraint with a twist. Top performer, 344k vph.
2. `What if this {thing} is actually {danger} ☠️ #{S}`: curiosity plus a mild threat, 189k vph.
3. `{S} {challenge name}!`: a named challenge, 149k vph.
4. `{Guest}'s aura has {S} shocked 😭`: guest or aura reaction, 75k vph.
5. `{S} explains why he never {quirk} 🤯`: quirky explainer, 72k vph.
6. `No way {family/surprise} came on stream ❤️‍🩹 #{S}`: wholesome surprise, 43k vph.
7. `{S}'s last {X} 😭🙏`: short and punchy, 32k vph.

## Format rules
- **Length:** 15-30s is the default. 46-60s is for story or emotional moments. Avoid 31-45s (weakest bucket).
- **Title:** short, lowercase feel, one closing emoji (😭 🤯 🥶 ☠️ 💀). Add #{streamer} on clipper-style titles.
- **Hook:** the payoff or the weird setup must show in the first 2 seconds.
- **Themes that work:**
  - Speed: animals, kids or fans, family, challenges, "aura" moments.
  - Kai: celebrity guests, quirky explainers, funny fails (8-17s cuts).
  - Football (Ronaldo/Mbappe) crossover lines from Speed: steady 13-17k vph.
