# Format Library (updated 2026-11-04)

## Title formulas (emoji at the end, lowercase/casual tone)
- `Bro was NOT {playing around / ready for X} 💀💀` – top performer, 94k vph peak
- `Bro HAD TO {get out of there} 😭🙏`
- `That {kick/play/line} was personal lowk 💀💀`
- `{Streamer} humbled {Name} fr 🥶`
- `{Streamer} is furious at {Name} for {reason} 💀` / `{Streamer} got cooked on {Platform}😂`
- `{Streamer} vs {Name} {challenge}` – for contests and events
- Emoji set that works: 💀 😭 🥶 🙏 😂. Add `#ishowspeed` only for Speed clips.

## Content angles
- Speed: sports and challenge moments, Spider-Man/Speeder-Man bits, Speed Goes Pro, giveaway or event news.
- Kai Cenat / stableronaldo: short drama and reaction moments (8-22s), such as someone getting called out or being upset.
- Reaction beats: a sudden escape, a hit or kick, someone getting humbled, an 'aura' moment.

## Length
- Target 15-45s. 31-45s showed the best average (about 39k vph) and <=15s was also strong. 16-30s is the bulk of what is posted.
- Drama and reaction clips can be as short as 8-20s. Stay under 60s unless it is a trailer or event.

## Posting windows (tentative)
- Primary: 10:00-14:00 UTC (07:00-11:00 Asuncion).
- Test: 22:00 UTC (19:00 Asuncion).
- Avoid 03, 09 and 23-00 UTC until data says otherwise.

## Source streamers
- Active: IShowSpeed.
- Add / test: stableronaldo, Kai Cenat, CaseOh.
- Watch: gaming and funnyclips tags (weak signal).

## Rules
- Metadata only: never download or re-upload competitor videos. Clip from our own sources.
- Retire topical hooks (Halloween, Prowler) once they fade, and keep the evergreen 'bro...' formulas.
