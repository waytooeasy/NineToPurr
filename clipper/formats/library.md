- Alt: {Celebrity}'s aura has {streamer} shocked 😭 (67k vph) | Bro does it smoothly at the end 🥶 (61k vph, early data)

## Title style
- Short, lowercase-feeling curiosity or reaction line plus one emoji (😭 🤯 🥶 ☠️).
- Put #{streamer} at the end for Speed clips.
- Use "No way...", "What if...", or a "but..." twist as the opener.

## Length
- Speed and story clips: 46-60s (best avg, small sample).
- Single-beat funny moments (Kai style): 8-30s.
- Avoid 31-45s unless the content is strong (weakest bucket).
- Over 60s only for trailers or announcements (26k vph).

## Post times
- 12:00-14:30 UTC (09:00-11:30 Asuncion, UTC-3) is the main window.
- Secondary, low data: 23 UTC, 09 UTC.
- Avoid 02-08 UTC and 16-21 UTC.

## Content angles that work
- Escalating stunts and challenges.
- Wholesome or funny surprise guests (siblings, kids, celebrities).
- "Aura" moments.
- Animal or danger curiosity.
- Kai Cenat: celebrity interactions and one-line gags.

## Streamer priorities

## Rules
- Our own clips only. Take pattern ideas from metadata, never reuse competitor videos.
