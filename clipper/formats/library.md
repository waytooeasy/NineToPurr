# Format Library (updated 2026-10-20)

## Length
- Target 8-30s. Hard cap 30s unless the moment needs it.
- Avoid 31-45s (weakest bucket, 17.9k avg vph). 46-60s works only for story moments (n=3, small sample).

## Title rules
- Under 10 words. One emoji at the end: 😭 🤯 🥶 🔥.
- Put the streamer name first, or use #streamer on recap-style titles.
- Use "aura", "no way", and "W {Streamer}" as proven words.

## Hook templates
1. {Streamer} explains why he never {quirky habit} 🤯
2. {Guest/celebrity}'s aura has {Streamer} shocked 😭
3. No way {family/guest} came on stream ❤️‍🩹 #{streamer}
4. {Streamer} tried to {mundane task} and it went wrong 🎤
5. Thats why we call him W {Streamer} 😭

## Moment types that work
- Reaction to a celebrity or guest (aura, shock).
- Quirky personal rule or habit explained.
- Family or guest surprise on stream.
- Challenge or game stakes (silence challenge, hardcore world, FC rankings).
- Short funny fail in a real-world setting (ordering food).
- Seasonal or anticipation lines ("can't wait for Christmas").

## Edit
- Open on the payoff or reaction in the first 1-2s, then give context.
- Keep captions big, with no dead air.
- Loop-friendly ending on clips under 20s.

## Post times
- Prime: 13:00-15:00 UTC (10:00-12:00 Asuncion).
- Secondary: 12:00 UTC (09:00 Asuncion).
- Test: 23:00 UTC (20:00 Asuncion).
- Avoid 04-08 UTC.
