# Format Library (updated 2026-11-25)

Rule: copy structure, not wording. Use our own clips and titles. Never reuse competitor videos.

## Title patterns (keep what works)
- `{Streamer} trusted the WRONG {thing} 😭💀`
- `{Streamer} went to {do good thing}... then {twist} 💀😭`
- `{Streamer} Finds Out {news} 💔`
- `{Streamer} was left speechless after watching {topic} 💔`
- `Bro went through EVERY emotion 💀💀`
- `{Streamer} got humbled on {game} 😭` (topical game reaction)
- `{Streamer} thought {X} died... 😢💔` (fake-out stakes)
- Style: 1-2 emojis (💀😭💔), one CAPS word for emphasis, an ellipsis before a twist. Add a #streamer tag only when the clip is about them.

## Length
- Default 20-30s: setup in the first 3s, payoff at the end.
- 9-12s for pure drama or reveal clips.
- 46-60s only when the clip has a real arc. Test it, since the evidence is skewed by one outlier.
- Avoid over 60s.

## Story shape
1. Hook line in the title and the first second (the reaction face or the reveal setup).
2. One twist or escalation.
3. End on the reaction or punchline. Cut the dead air after it.
