# Format Library (updated 2026-11-05)
_Copy structure, never competitor titles or footage. Metadata-informed only._

## Title/hook templates
1. `Bro {was NOT ready for / HAD TO get out of} {thing} 💀` – top performer, reaction to a wild moment (56-71k vph)
2. `That {moment} was personal lowk 💀💀` – physical or heated play (57k)
3. `{Streamer} humbled {guest} fr 🥶` – win or clap-back (43k)
4. `{Streamer} vs {Name} {challenge}` – quick contest (36k)
5. `{Streamer} is {furious/sad} at {person} for {petty reason} 🥹` or `thinks {fake thing} is real 😂` – Kai-style drama (10-15k)

## Format rules
- Length: 20-35s default. Use <=15s for pure contests or one-beat gags. Go 40-45s only if there is a clear payoff.
- Open on the peak moment in the first second, then show the context.
- Title: lowercase-casual, 1-2 emoji (💀 😭 🥶), 8 words or fewer, no hashtags except `#ishowspeed`-style when it is the only text.
- Pair streamers with their recurring foils (Speed x Jamal, Kai x chat guests) for repeat engagement.
