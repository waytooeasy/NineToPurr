# Format Library (updated 2026-10-20)

_Based on a small sample (20 Shorts, mostly IShowSpeed). Re-check when more data comes in._

## Title rules
- Short, lowercase-casual, caption-style. It reads like a friend reacting.
- End with 1-2 emojis. Proven: 😭 🙏 ❤️‍🩹 🥶 😂 💀.
- Add #{streamer} on emotional or wholesome clips. It is on 4 of the top 6.
- Name the payoff or conflict, and never describe the setup.
- Use "Bro", "Nah", "No way" and "He really said" openers.

## Hook templates
1. `Bro found the one on camera 😭❤️‍🩹 #{streamer}` (emotional payoff)
2. `No way his {sibling/family} came on stream ❤️‍🩹 #{streamer}` (surprise guest)
3. `{Streamer}'s last {bark/words/move} 😭🙏` (mini-ending)
4. `The {villager/NPC/guy} had a personal problem with {streamer}😂` (character conflict)
5. `What did {name} say at the end?😂` (curiosity gap)

## Clip types that work
- Wholesome or emotional moments: family, guests, supportive relatives, "aura" moments.
- Animals and absurd mini-stories: the dog, "last bark".
- Minecraft/hardcore stakes: risking a world on one move, villager conflict.
- Football crossovers: Ronaldo, Mbappe references.
- Hype or underdog wins: "HOW DID {S} WIN THAT?!".
- Superlative punchlines: "{S} is the most talented guy ever🥶".

## Structure
- Open on the payoff or reaction within the first 1-2 seconds.
- Keep the clip to one beat. Duration data is unknown, so test 15-35s and log it.
- Loop-friendly endings and curiosity gaps ("what did X say at the end?") invite rewatches.

## Post times
- **Primary:** 11-13 UTC (08:00-10:00 Asuncion), best at 13 UTC (10:00 Asuncion).
- **Secondary:** 02-05 UTC (23:00-02:00 Asuncion).
- **Avoid:** 10, 15, 20 and 23 UTC.
