# Nine to Purr — series bible

The writers' room reads this on every run (prompt-cached). HQ owns it; the weekly learn step proposes edits, Lucas or HQ accepts them. Template: [`../_template/BIBLE.md`](../_template/BIBLE.md).

## 1. Logline
Three coworkers at Clawporate Inc. survive the 9-to-5: a lazy cat who somehow wins, an overachiever who somehow loses, and a pigeon boss who calls a meeting about it.

## 2. World
| Place | Visual line (reuse in prompts) |
|---|---|
| Clawporate open-plan office | grey carpet, rows of desks with monitors, potted plants, big window light from the left, a sad coffee machine |
| Meeting room | glass walls, whiteboard full of arrows, long table, one flickering ceiling light |
| Mochi's apartment | tiny studio, unmade bed, stacks of instant-noodle cups, laptop, alarm clock |
| The commute | city bus interior at morning rush, rain on the windows |
| Corner store | fluorescent light at night, snack aisle |

Time follows the real calendar: Mondays, payday (15th and 30th), Thanksgiving week, year-end reviews, holidays.

## 4. Look
- Style line in every shot prompt: *"cinematic 3D animated film, soft realistic fur and feathers, natural window light, muted office palette (grey, beige, soft blue), shallow depth of field, real-world adult office, 9:16 vertical"*.
- Banned: toddler-cartoon look, neon kids' colours, chibi proportions, readable text inside the generated picture, real logos or brands, photoreal humans.

## 5. Sound
- Wordless: sighs, meows with attitude, pigeon coos, gasps, snores.
- Office sounds: keyboard clacks, printer jams, coffee machine gurgle, a generic chat ping, phone ring.
- Light original ambience only. No outside music, no trending sounds, no dialogue.

## 6. Humor rules
- One situation, one turn, one payoff. The payoff lands in the last 2–3 seconds.
- Running gags: Mochi wins by doing nothing; Pearl's effort backfires; Gus's meetings; the CEO's phone.
- "Send this to a coworker" topics: meetings, emails, deadlines, Mondays, payday, performance reviews, the printer, working from home, 5:00 pm, team building.
- Punch at situations, never at real groups. No real people, brands, politics or religion.
- Banned: babies or kids, animal distress, injury or rescue, gore, sexual or cheating tropes, shock, bathroom humor as the main joke.

## 7. On-screen text
- Hook in the first 1.5 s, ≤7 words, plain words, no emoji.
- Thoughts and lines as short cards, ≤5 words each, inside the 9:16 safe zone, never over a face.
- Caption look follows today's `formats/style.json` (radar).

## 8. Titles and metadata
- ≤60 characters, relatable workday phrasing in today's popping pattern, names a character when it helps. Never misleading.
- Description: one line + `#NineToPurr #officehumor #shorts`.
- `selfDeclaredMadeForKids=false`. `containsSyntheticMedia=false` (animated, clearly not real) unless a shot looks photoreal.
- Category 23 (Comedy).
